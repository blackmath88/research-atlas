The pipeline tests cover duplicate IDs, namesakes, missing author IDs and missing abstracts. They cover empty and missing reference lists and external reference stubs that get no fabricated links. They check that large author lists are not expanded, that there are no dangling IDs, that provenance is complete, and that shared-reference computation is exact. They check that conversation candidates have real paths and exclude coauthors, that the offline rebuild is byte-identical, and that the committed fixture export is up to date. On the client side they cover Bearer-only credentials, which never appear in URLs, logs or cache files. They also cover retry, cache and offline resume; the exhausted budget stopping after one request; `auth_blocked`, `cap_reached` and keyless `partial` statuses; and that public data contains no credentials and no synthetic records.
